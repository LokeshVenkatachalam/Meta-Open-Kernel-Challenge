01 · Problem

What this kernel asked for.

The retained prefill kernel is adapted/reused public work from team keell0renz and its vjeux, alygg77, and ZhimaoL lineages. It is not presented as an independently original kernel.

The exact retained source beat SGLang by 1.9191900674402353x and placed 107 of 898 in the current provisional snapshot, behind a 2.1471x leader.

02 · Retained approach

What survived the campaign.

The integration combines ZhimaoL's N72 FP8-query FMHA, pinned cuBLASLt dense projection plans, fused layer boundaries, and a persistent grouped W4A16 implementation that losslessly repacks NVFP4 and dequantizes into BF16 WGMMA operands.

The publication preserves the exact upstream-attributed bytes, full Apache-2.0 license, integrated NOTICE, and transitively applicable NathenFernandes MIT notice text.

03 · Measured lessons

What the numbers taught us.

The public N72 FMHA and LT2 integration raised the campaign from a first scored 1.7859x result to 1.9192x.

Long full-context attention improved materially, leaving the persistent grouped expert GEMM as the dominant measured primitive on the largest token counts.

Later tuning checks were regressions and are documented as such rather than relabeled as winners.

04 · Experiments that did not survive

Regression is part of the record.

Spread expert tile ordering on the retained base reached 1.9069x, below the exact public source.

A fresh-thread context wrapper reached 1.9023x, and separating the large-M expert phases reached 1.8915x; neither beat the retained source.

05 · Boundaries

Where this result stops.

This source is public licensed reuse, not an original-authorship claim. The upstream copyright and attribution notices remain controlling for incorporated portions.

The campaign did not prove a new local tuning improvement over the retained keell0renz source, and the ranking is provisional.
